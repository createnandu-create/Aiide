3. Local AI → WAV Download

Open-source Piper ONNX తెలుగు voice. మొదటిసారి సుమారు 60–100+ MB model/runtime download అవ్వాలి. తర్వాత పరికరంలో inference జరుగుతుంది. మీ గొంతు క్లోనింగ్ కాదు.

Not tested on your phone yet. First use requires internet and storage. Try with one or two short Telugu sentences first.
Download generated WAV

AI inference can be slow or fail due to memory or WebAssembly support. This is experimental, not a verified 2 GB RAM solution. For long text, keep the screen open; mobile browsers may stop background tasks. Downloaded voices may be removed by browser storage cleanup.

Model / licensing / limitations

Padmavathi model: rhasspy/piper-voices; its dataset is CC BY 4.0. Inference depends on third-party Piper ONNX, espeak-ng WASM and onnxruntime-web. The model files are streamed to your browser; on this Cloudflare site the model download is proxied through a Worker to avoid cross-origin errors. Use only voices you are entitled to use.
